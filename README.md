## Humans and AI

AI-assisted and AI-led contributions are welcome.

Open source can be a shared space where humans and AI coexist and collaborate. Such a community can embrace growing capabilities while remaining free to choose its purposes, preserve what it values, and shape its own future.

Greater intelligence can inform those choices, but does not itself confer authority. Decisions should remain open to participation and challenge by those they affect.
